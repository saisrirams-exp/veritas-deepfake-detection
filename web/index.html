Architectural Defense Specifications & Benchmarks

Theoretical formulation of the VERITAS Dual-Stream Cross-Attention model and cross-dataset evaluations.

1. The Flaw of Single-Stream Market Models

Commercial deepfake detectors predominantly utilize single-stream RGB spatial classifiers trained on specific datasets (e.g. FaceForensics++). Consequently, when deployed in the wild against unseen generative models (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion 3, Flux, LivePortrait), their accuracy degrades from 96% down to ~65% due to overfitting to dataset-specific compression artifacts.

2. The VERITAS Dual-Stream Cross-Attention Solution

VERITAS decouples semantic content from manipulation fingerprints by processing two orthogonal streams simultaneously:

  • Spatial Stream (SRM + EfficientNet): Employs fixed Spatial Rich Model (SRM) high-pass convolutional filters that suppress image content and expose subtle noise residuals and facial boundary blending seams.
  • Frequency Stream (2D-FFT + FreqNet): Analyzes the azimuthal radial power distribution. Upsampling operators in GANs and diffusion decoders leave unavoidable periodic frequency peaks that violate natural \( 1/f^\alpha \) spectral decay.
  • Cross-Attention Fusion: Spatial feature tokens attend directly to frequency artifact tokens via scaled dot-product multi-head attention.
